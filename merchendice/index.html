QUESTIONS

Before you enquire

What do you do?

We build and run a custom merch store for creators. The store is on Shopify. Clothing is produced on demand with Printify, and delicate jewelry with ShineOn. We handle production and shipping, so you don’t keep stock.

Who is it for?

Creators who want their own merch store, mainly in the US, without holding inventory or handling production and shipping themselves.

What do you need from me?

Your name, email, and a short description of what you have in mind. Links or handles for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or other platforms are optional. They help us find your work.

How do production and shipping work?

When someone orders, the item is made to order and shipped to them. Clothing goes through Printify. Delicate jewelry goes through ShineOn. You don’t pack boxes or hold inventory.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry, or email merchendices@gmail.com. We’ll reply by email. Pricing and other terms aren’t listed on this site. We’ll go through them on a call or by email after you enquire.

Questions first? Email merchendices@gmail.com.
