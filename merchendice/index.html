How it works

It starts with a short message. You keep posting while we do the rest, and we check in with you at each stage.

  1. Idea

    Send a few lines about your channel and what you picture. Start with any idea, even just a phrase or a joke.

  2. Designs

    We draw original artwork from your content and colors, and adjust it with you until it feels completely yours.

  3. Samples

    Physical samples of the products you picked come first, so you can judge the print, color, and material in your hands.

  4. Your store

    A custom Shopify store with product pages and shipping set up. You can click through every page before it opens.

  5. Launch

    You share the link. Each order is printed on demand and shipped straight to the buyer.

We only launch products that pass our sample check.

We point you towards items that print well, and nothing launches until you’ve signed it off.

FAQ

Who is this for?

Creators of every size, on any topic. What matters is an audience that knows your style.

How involved do I need to be?

As involved as you like. Some creators want a say in every detail, others would rather react to a few options. Both work.

How do we choose the products?

We look at what you make and suggest a few directions together.

What does it cost to get started?

There’s nothing to pay while we shape the idea, design and sample. You only pay once your store is ready to launch. Commercial terms are agreed with you before any work begins.
