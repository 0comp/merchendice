How it works

It starts with a short message. You keep posting while we do the rest, and we check in with you at each stage.

  1. Idea

    Tell us about your channel and what you picture, even if it’s only a feeling or a running joke from your comments. We reply with questions and a few directions you can react to.

  2. Designs

    We draw original artwork from your content, colours and in-jokes, and keep adjusting it until it reads as yours rather than ours.

  3. Samples

    Physical samples of the products you chose are made first. If the print or the material isn’t right once you see it for real, we change it.

  4. Your store

    A custom Shopify store in your look, with the product pages and shipping set up. You can click through every page before it opens.

  5. Launch

    You share the link. Each order is printed on demand and shipped straight to the buyer, and we keep looking after the store once it’s live.

“Will my merch look cheap or off-brand?”

It’s the right thing to worry about. Your name is on it, and people notice a blurry print or a logo dropped onto a blank.

No generic templates. Every design starts from what you actually post, and we don’t put your handle on a stock graphic and call it a collection. Nothing launches until you’ve said yes to it, from the first sketch to the store’s home page.

We’ll also steer you away from products that print badly. Some items look fine on screen and disappointing in hand, and we’d rather tell you that before you get attached to one.

Questions

Something we haven’t covered? Contact us.

Do I need a big following?

No. An audience that cares about what you do matters more than follower count. Tell us a bit about yours and we’ll be honest about whether merch makes sense right now.

What kind of creators do you work with?

Any topic. Gaming, cooking, fitness, music, comedy, or something nobody else makes videos about. What matters is that your audience already asks where they can buy something from you.

How involved do I need to be?

As involved as you like. Some creators want a say in every detail, others would rather react to a few options. Both work.

I don’t know what merch to make. Can you help?

Yes, and that’s a normal place to start. We look at what you make and come back with a few directions. You pick the one that feels right.

What does it cost to get started?

There’s nothing to pay while we shape the idea, design and sample. You only pay once your store is ready to launch. Commercial terms are agreed with you before any work begins.

Got an idea, or just a channel?

Write a few lines about what you make and what you’d like to sell. We reply by email, usually with a couple of questions.
