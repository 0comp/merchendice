How it works

From a short note to an open store.

We take care of everything after your first message, so you can keep making content.

  1. Idea

    Your brand, your audience and what you’re picturing.

  2. Designs

    Artwork drawn for your brand, shaped together with you.

  3. Samples

    Physical samples in the chosen products and colours, before anything goes on sale.

  4. Your store

    A custom Shopify store in your look, set up for you.

  5. Launch

    Your store opens to your audience. Printing and delivery are handled for you.

Quality and brand fit

“Will my merch look cheap or off-brand?”

Your name is on it, so it’s the right thing to ask.

  • No generic templates

    Every design starts from your content, your colours and your voice. Nothing is a stock layout with your name swapped in.

  • You sign off on everything

    Each design, the products and the store. If something isn’t right, it doesn’t launch.

  • Products that fit your brand

    We help you pick what suits you and your audience, not what’s easiest to print.

Questions

Before you write.

Still wondering about something? Contact us.

Do I need a big following?

No. An audience that cares about what you do matters more than follower count. Tell us a bit about yours and we’ll be honest about whether merch makes sense right now.

How involved do I need to be?

As involved as you like. Some creators shape every detail, others prefer to react to a few options. Both work well.

I don’t know what merch to make. Can you help?

Yes. That’s a normal place to start. We come back with a few directions based on what you make. You pick what feels right.

What does it cost to get started?

There’s nothing to pay while we shape the idea, design and sample. You only pay once your store is ready to launch. Commercial terms are agreed with you before any work begins.
