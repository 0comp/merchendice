How it works

It starts with a short message. We take it from there.

  1. Idea

    We read your message and look at your channel. Any idea is enough to start from, even just a phrase or a joke.

  2. Designs

    We draw exclusive designs from your content and colours.

  3. Products

    We pick the products that suit your designs and your audience, from clothing and totes to mugs and jewellery.

  4. Your store

    We build a custom store with product pages and checkout set up.

  5. Launch

    The store opens and we look after it. Every order is printed on demand and delivered to the buyer.

What you get

A store in your look and a first collection drawn from your content. Nothing to pay while we shape the idea and the designs, and one launch fee when your store is ready to open. Every month you get a report and a payout, so you always know what sold. You can order samples at cost to wear, film or check first. All we ask is at least 3 videos featuring the merch, so your audience knows it's there.

Frequently asked questions

How does Merchendice work?

We design every piece from a creator’s own content and colours, so the designs are exclusive to them. Then we build and run the store. Each order is printed on demand and shipped for them, so they never touch stock. They have as much say as they want, and we handle the rest.

Who is it for?

Creators of any size, on any platform.

What can I sell?

Lots of things: clothing, totes and bags, mugs, home goods like pillows and blankets, and jewellery.

What do I need to do?

Very little. Tell us what you like, approve the designs, and share your store with your audience. We take care of everything else.

How do I get started?

Send us the form, and we’ll reply by email, usually within one or two days.

How do we choose the products?

We look at what you make and who watches it, and choose a range that fits both.

What does it cost to get started?

There’s nothing to pay while we shape the idea and the designs. You only pay once your store is ready to launch. Commercial terms are agreed with you before any work begins.

How long until my store is live?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks.

Where do you ship?

We ship to the US, Canada and most countries worldwide. Right now we don’t ship to the EU or the UK.

What if an order arrives damaged?

Our supplier is responsible for getting it right. If something arrives damaged or faulty, send us a photo and we’ll replace it.
