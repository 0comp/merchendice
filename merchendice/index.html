How it works

It starts with a short message. You keep posting while we do the rest, and we check in with you at each stage.

  1. Idea

    Send a few lines about your channel and what you picture. Rough is fine.

  2. Designs

    We draw original artwork from your content and colours, and adjust it with you until it reads as yours rather than ours.

  3. Samples

    Physical samples of the products you picked come first, so you can judge the print, colour and material in your hands.

  4. Your store

    A custom Shopify store with product pages and shipping set up. You can click through every page before it opens.

  5. Launch

    You share the link. Each order is printed on demand and shipped straight to the buyer.

We’d rather say no to a product than sell a bad one.

We steer you away from items that print badly, and nothing launches until you’ve signed it off.

Questions

Do I need a big following?

No. An audience that cares about what you do matters more than follower count. Tell us a bit about yours and we’ll be honest about whether merch makes sense right now.

What kind of creators do you work with?

Any topic. Gaming, cooking, fitness, music, comedy, or something nobody else makes videos about. What matters is that your audience would be glad to own something from you.

How involved do I need to be?

As involved as you like. Some creators want a say in every detail, others would rather react to a few options. Both work.

I don’t know what merch to make. Can you help?

Yes, and that’s a normal place to start. We look at what you make and come back with a few directions. You pick the one that feels right.

What does it cost to get started?

There’s nothing to pay while we shape the idea, design and sample. You only pay once your store is ready to launch. Commercial terms are agreed with you before any work begins.
