QUESTIONS

Before you write

What do you do?

We help creators turn their own ideas into merch. We shape the idea with you, design the products and the storefront, and coordinate production and delivery. The storefront is a Shopify store. You approve each step.

Who is it for?

YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram creators, with fashion, beauty, and lifestyle as the focus.

What do you need from me?

A short note: your name, email, and the idea. Links or handles for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or other platforms are optional. They help us find your work.

How do production and delivery work?

We coordinate print-on-demand production and delivery. Delicate jewelry is a lead direction, with clothing and totes as well. Commercial terms are agreed before any work begins.

How do I get started?

Send us a short note about your idea at this site, or email merchendices@gmail.com. We’ll reply by email. Commercial terms aren’t published here. They are agreed before any work begins.

Questions first? Email merchendices@gmail.com.
