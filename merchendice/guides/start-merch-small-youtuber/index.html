How to start merch as a small YouTuber

You do not need a huge audience to think about merch. If people already recognise a joke, a phrase or a look from your videos, that is enough to start from. This guide explains what starting merch actually involves when someone else designs the pieces, builds the store and handles the orders.

A small channel is not a barrier

Merchendice is a merch studio for creators and influencers of any size, on any platform, including YouTube. There is no subscriber count to pass before you can send a message. The useful test is simpler: is there something your viewers would recognise as yours?

That might be a running joke, a colour palette you always use or a phrase people already repeat in the comments. You do not need finished artwork. You do not need to know which products to sell yet.

What you actually have to do

The short version is: very little. You tell us what you like, approve the designs, and share your store with your audience. We take care of everything else.

In practice it looks like this.

  1. Send a short message. A few lines is enough: who you are, where you post and what you are picturing, even if it is still rough. Links to your channel are helpful but optional. We reply by email, usually within one or two days. You can start from the enquiry form.
  2. Talk through the idea. We read your message and look at your channel. Any idea is enough to start from, even just a phrase or a joke. You have as much say as you want.
  3. Approve the designs. We draw exclusive designs from your content and colours, so they are made for you and for your audience. Nothing goes into the store until you are happy with it.
  4. Look at the range. We pick a small set of products that suit the designs and the people who watch you. That can include clothing, totes and bags, mugs, home goods like pillows and blankets, and jewellery. A small range, chosen carefully, usually carries a design better than putting it on everything.
  5. Share the store. We build a custom store with product pages and checkout, then keep it running. When it opens, your part is to tell your audience it exists. We ask for at least 3 videos featuring the merch, so people can see it in the same place they already watch you. You can order samples at cost first, to wear, film or check.

A store is usually live in 2 to 3 weeks.

If you want to see the finished standard before you write, you can click around the example store. It is a demo shop for a made-up brand, with a collection page, quick view and a bag.

You never have to hold stock

Each order is printed on demand and shipped for you, so you never touch stock. You do not buy a box of shirts in advance, store them at home or post them yourself. When someone orders, the item is printed and delivered to them.

If something arrives damaged or faulty, the buyer can send a photo and we will replace it. Our supplier is responsible for getting the order right.

What you get, in plain terms

You get a store in your look and a first collection drawn from your content. We build the store and keep it running.

There is nothing to pay while we shape the idea and the designs, and one launch fee when your store is ready to open. Commercial terms are agreed with you before any work begins. Every month you get a report and a payout, so you can see what sold. We do not publish prices here; those are part of the conversation when you get in touch.

Where the merch can ship

This matters if your viewers are mostly in one place. We ship to the US, Canada and most countries worldwide. Right now we do not ship to the EU or the UK.

If a large part of your audience is in the EU or the UK, say so when you write. We would rather be clear about that at the start than have you open a store your viewers cannot order from.

A few things this guide is not promising

Starting merch does not guarantee sales. A small, loyal audience may buy, or they may not. We will not pretend otherwise.

You also do not need to design the artwork yourself, learn a print-on-demand dashboard or run customer support for every order. Those are the parts we handle. Your job is the part only you can do: know your channel, approve what looks like you and tell your viewers the store is there.

How to get started

If this sounds like the right shape for your channel, send a few lines about yourself and what you have in mind. We will reply by email, usually within one or two days.
