Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the details you send through the enquiry form on merchendice.com, and what the site stores in your browser.

What the enquiry form collects

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any platforms you tick: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or other platforms (optional)
  • Channel links or handles you add for those platforms (optional)
  • Your project description

The form also has a hidden field that people don’t see or fill in. It catches automated spam. If it contains anything, the submission is ignored and not sent.

To limit spam, the site counts how many enquiries come from each network address (IP address). That count is kept in memory for about ten minutes and the site doesn’t save it.

Like most websites, the web server in front of the site may keep standard access logs, such as IP address, time and page requested, for security and troubleshooting.

Why it’s collected

Only so we can read your enquiry and reply to you by email about your merch idea. It isn’t added to a mailing list, and the form never asks for payment details.

Where it goes

Your enquiry is sent as an email to the Merchendice inbox, with your email address set as the reply-to address so we can answer you. The email is delivered through an email service provider that handles it on our behalf. The site itself doesn’t store enquiries in a database.

How long it’s kept

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as they’re needed to handle your enquiry and any work that follows from it.

Cookie on the storefront directory

On the storefront directory, the site may store a cookie named merchendice_storefront_view. It remembers whether you were looking at example or real storefronts, for up to one year. It isn’t used for tracking or advertising, and you can remove it in your browser at any time. The site uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to have them deleted. Contact us through the enquiry form and say what you’d like us to do.
