MERCHENDICE

Privacy notice

This draft describes what the enquiry form on merchendice.com collects and why. It was written for review on November 17, 2026. No effective date is set.

What the enquiry form collects

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any platforms you select: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or other platforms (optional)
  • Channel links or handles you type for those platforms (optional)
  • Your project description

The form also includes a hidden field labeled Website. You are not asked to fill it in. It is there to catch automated submissions. If that field contains anything, the submission is ignored and is not emailed.

Why we collect it

So Merchendice can read your enquiry and reply by email about building and running a merch store. The form is not a mailing list, and it does not ask for payment details.

Where it goes

A normal submission is emailed to merchendices@gmail.com. The email address you enter is used as the reply-to address. The owner still needs to name the email provider and say how long those messages are kept. This draft does not set a retention period.

Cookie on the storefront directory

On the storefront directory, the site may store a cookie named merchendice_storefront_view in your browser. It remembers whether you were looking at example or real storefronts, for up to one year. It is not used for advertising. You can remove it in your browser.

What this draft deliberately leaves out

  • No legal entity, postal address, or registration number. Those details have not been supplied.
  • No completed US state privacy notice, and no claim about any particular privacy law.
  • No description of hosting or server logs. Add that if the host keeps them.

Contact

Questions about this notice: merchendices@gmail.com.
