Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to the details you send through the message form on merchendice.com.

Who is responsible

Oscar Thomas, co-founder of Merchendice, is the data controller for the details described here. To reach him about your data, use the message form.

What the message form collects

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Links to your channels, if you add them (optional)
  • Your idea

The form also has a hidden field that people don’t see or fill in. It catches automated spam. If it contains anything, the submission is ignored and not sent.

To limit spam, the site counts how many messages come from each IP address. That count is kept in memory for about ten minutes and the site doesn’t save it.

Like most websites, the web server and proxy in front of the site keep standard access logs, such as IP address, time, and page requested, for security and troubleshooting. These logs are deleted after 30 days.

Why it’s collected

Only so we can read your message and reply to you by email about your merch idea. It isn’t added to a mailing list, and the form never asks for payment details.

Where it goes

Your message is sent as an email to the Merchendice inbox, with your email address set as the reply-to address so we can answer you. The email is delivered through an email service provider that handles it on our behalf. The site itself doesn’t store messages in a database.

How long it’s kept

Emails with your message are kept only as long as they’re needed to reply and to handle any work that follows from it. Server and proxy logs are kept for 30 days.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools.

While you fill in the form, your browser keeps a draft of it in local storage on your own device, so it isn’t lost if you leave the page. The draft isn’t sent anywhere until you press Send message, and it’s deleted once your message has been sent.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to have them deleted. Send us a message and say what you’d like us to do.
